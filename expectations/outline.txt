# section of this suite (the name in its `Section:` line), and a Page is a hand-written file in

# no part names goes into a last part, "More".

# section is: Flying a VTOL and the radio sections are parts of the ground station in the
# suite, and chapters of Learn to fly here.

# stays, under "For developers"), and Next goes from tutorial to tutorial.
